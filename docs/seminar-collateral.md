## The scale rule

**1 ft = 96 px. 1 inch = 8 px exactly. The artwork is 1:12.**

Everything is vector, so the printer scales to the real size with no loss.
**Give the printer the size in FEET, never the pixel size of the file.** A
6-inch cap height on the finished banner is 48 px in the artwork.

Only the RATIO is baked in. If any panel turns out to be a different size in the
same proportion, nothing needs redrawing.

|---|---|---|---|
| Main backdrop | 16 × 8 ft | 1536 × 768 | 2:1 |
| Welcome banner | 8 × 8 ft | 768 × 768 | 1:1 |

## The one rule that shaped the backdrop

**On a 16 × 8 ft backdrop, people on stage block the bottom 5.5 ft.** Only the
top 2.5 ft is reliably in shot.

So the logo and the date sit in that top band — that is what every photograph
catches — the message sits mid-height where it reads above and between people,
and the bottom 2 ft carries only a candlestick texture and the domain repeated
left **and** right, so one of the two survives whoever is standing there.

The same logic puts the podium mark in the upper half: the foot of a podium
panel is lost to shadow and to the speaker's own body in almost every frame.

## What is deliberately NOT on any panel

- **No GMP / expected premium.** It is unofficial, it moves daily, and a figure
  printed on a wall is wrong by the time the hall opens.
- **No "apply now", no ratings, no recommendation.** We distribute and inform;
  we hold no investment-adviser registration, so the copy states facts and
  never advises.
- **No app-store badge.** There is no store listing behind it yet.
- **No support email or phone.** None is published, and inventing one on a
  banner is worse than leaving it off. Add real ones when they exist.

## Print notes

- **Bleed** — ask for 2 inch extra all round for hem and eyelets. The artwork
  already keeps a 4–11 inch margin inside every edge, so nothing is at risk.
- **Colour** — indigo `#3c2e7e` ≈ C85 M90 Y20 K10, gold `#ffcb32` ≈ C0 M20 Y85
  K0. **Ask for a proof of the indigo.** A deep violet-indigo is the single
  most likely colour to go muddy on flex; the candlestick texture on the lower
  half is there partly to break up a flat flood and hide roller banding.
- **Material** — star flex for the backdrop and dias; vinyl on sunboard, or
  roll-up, for the standees and podium.
- **Heights are measured, never estimated.** `scratchpad/seminar/measure.js`
  renders an artboard in headless Chrome and reports the lowest painted TEXT
  edge against the frame. It earned itself immediately: standee 5 ran **33 px**
  past the frame and standee 1 by **4 px**, and because the board is
  `overflow: hidden` both looked finished on screen while dropping
  *investoyard.com* and the company name off the bottom. **Re-run it after any
  copy change** — a line of text added at the top pushes the address off at the
  foot, silently.
- **Fonts** — Archivo (Latin) and Mukta (Devanagari + Latin), both Google
  Fonts, both free to embed. **Check the Devanagari renders** on the proof:
  कहीं भी / इधर ही must keep their matras.

## Decisions still open

1. **Event name.** Placeholder reads *IPO Investor Seminar*.
2. **[TIME] and [VENUE]** on the welcome banner.
3. **QR code** for the registration panel — generate for `investoyard.com` and
   drop it in the marked square.
4. **Allotment data covers exactly ONE IPO.** `AllotmentRecord` holds 2,26,552
   rows and every one belongs to a single issue. The checker works, but a
   visitor testing their PAN against anything else gets "no record". Standee 5
   claims the capability, not coverage — if you want the claim to land at the
   seminar, **import recent registrar files first**, and Jio's the day it
   allots (26 Oct).
5. **`startBid` is OFF for every IPO, Jio included.** So a visitor cannot apply
   by UPI on the platform today, which is why standee 2 says *pre-filled ASBA
   forms* and nothing about UPI. If Start Bid is switched on for JPL before the
   seminar, the APPLY line can become:
   *"By UPI mandate from your own bank, or a pre-filled ASBA form ready to print."*
   **Do not print the UPI line while the gate is off.**
