What is an IPO?

A company sells shares to the public for the first time, to raise money. Three things happen, in order.

1
The company sets a price band
Plus a lot size — the smallest number of shares you can apply for.
2
You apply during the open window
Usually three days. The money is blocked in your bank account, not debited.
3
Shares are allotted, then listed
If more people apply than there are shares, not everyone gets them.
