Allotment is a lottery.

Not first-come, first-served. Applying at 10am on day one does nothing that applying on the last afternoon doesn’t.

When retail is oversubscribed
The registrar runs a computerised draw. Each valid application is one entry, and the winners get one lot each.
Applying for more lots does not improve the odds of that one application being drawn — in the retail category it is one entry either way.

Didn’t get it? The block on your bank account is released automatically. The money never left.
